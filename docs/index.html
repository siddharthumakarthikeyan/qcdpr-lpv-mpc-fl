Real-Time LPV-MPC with Feedback Linearisation for a Quadrotor-Based Reconfigurable Cable-Driven Parallel Robot

Siddharth Umakarthikeyan, Badri Narayanan R

SASTRA Deemed to be University, India

Research code · 2025

QCDPR-LPV-MPC-FL: a feedback-linearisation and LPV-MPC cascade, a rate-limited tension QP and an online EKF control four quadrotors and four ground vehicles that carry one platform on eight cables. Render of the same system in NVIDIA Isaac Sim.

Abstract

We present a real-time control framework for a Quadrotor-based reconfigurable Cable-Driven Parallel Robot (QCDPR): four quadrotors and four differential-drive ground vehicles cooperatively suspend and manoeuvre a rigid end-effector platform through eight cables. The controller is a cascade of Feedback Linearisation (FL) and Linear Parameter Varying Model Predictive Control (LPV-MPC). An FL position loop generates thrust and attitude references for each quadrotor; a quasi-LPV attitude MPC re-linearises the quadrotor dynamics at every step; each ground vehicle runs a nonlinear MPC on formation targets obtained from the platform inverse kinematics; and a rate-limited quadratic programme distributes the eight cable tensions to realise the desired platform wrench.

Cable forces are fed back into every UAV and UGV model, the platform wrench is computed from the actual anchor forces, and a small alternating azimuthal skew of the anchors makes the 6×8 wrench matrix full row rank, so yaw is controlled through the cables themselves. An Extended Kalman Filter estimates the platform mass and external disturbance online. All MPC problems are solved as condensed QPs with direct OSQP calls. The system tracks quadrilateral, circular and sinusoidal paths with millimetre-level error and recovers from a 2 kg payload step, with the mass estimate converging within 1.0 s.

Method

Inverse kinematicsplatform pose → 4 UAV + 4 UGV targets
FL position loopper UAV: thrust, φ_d, θ_d
LPV-MPC attitudeper UAV, re-linearised each step
NL-MPCper UGV: velocity commands
Tension QPrate-limited OSQP, 8 cables, full wrench
Coupled dynamicsUAVs, UGVs and platform, RK4
EKFonline mass and disturbance
  • Outer loop: feedback linearisation. A feedback-linearising position controller turns the desired UAV acceleration into thrust and roll and pitch references, with the measured cable force as feed-forward (a_eff = a_cmd − F_cable / m_uav).
  • Inner loop: quasi-LPV attitude MPC. The quadrotor rotational dynamics are re-linearised at the current state each control step and tracked by an MPC over a 10-step horizon.
  • Ground vehicles: nonlinear MPC. Each differential-drive UGV tracks its formation position from the end-effector inverse kinematics over a 15-step horizon, with bidirectional velocity bounds so it can resist a cable pull while stationary.
  • Tension distribution. A rate-limited QP allocates the eight tensions to meet the full platform wrench at every cycle, with a Newton fallback. Desired roll, pitch and yaw torques come from PD laws on the platform, so attitude is held by the cables alone; the platform has no aerodynamic damping.
  • Rank-6 cable layout. An 8° alternating azimuthal skew of the anchors makes W ∈ ℝ6×8 full row rank, giving active yaw through the cables with zero net yaw torque under uniform tension.
  • Adaptive estimation. An EKF estimates the effective platform mass and an AR(1) disturbance online, and feeds both to the controller.
  • Solver. Every MPC is written as a condensed, input-only QP and solved by direct OSQP calls, without a modelling layer.

After a +2 kg payload step the EKF converges to within ±5% of the true mass in 1.0 s, with a steady-state mass error of 0.002 kg. Across the three trajectories, adaptation reduces mean tracking error by 82–91% against the non-adaptive baseline.

Reproduce

Python 3.10 or later with numpy, scipy, matplotlib and osqp. All results in this page are produced by one command (about 7 minutes on a laptop):

pip install -r requirements.txt
python generate_all_results.py --no-video

Single runs, adaptive mode and the ablation study:

python main.py circular
python main.py quadrilateral --adaptive --scenario payload_change
python main.py quadrilateral --ablation --scenario payload_change
python main.py circular --monitor
